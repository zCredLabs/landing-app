LEAD WORKFLOW

Admitting an Organization

1. Check: Verify credentials against program rules.
2. Decide: Return Eligible, Review Required, or Ineligible + expiry.
3. Enforce: Contract reads status and gates access.
TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Liability & Data Retention

Rule Owner: Institution defines policy thresholds.
Retention: Ephemeral memory; only audit hashes and decision metadata persist.
Revocation: Time-bound expiry + onchain revocation hook.
